# UNFCCC Conferences of Parties (COPs): Landmark Milestones

The **United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC)** was adopted at the 1992 Rio Earth Summit. The Conference of Parties (COP) serves as the supreme decision-making authority meeting annually.

## Major COPs at a Glance

| Conference | Year | Historic Outcome & Key Mechanisms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **COP3 (Kyoto)** | 1997 | **Kyoto Protocol**: Legally binding emission reduction targets for Annex I (developed) nations based on Common But Differentiated Responsibilities (CBDR). Clean Development Mechanism (CDM), Emission Trading. |
| **COP13 (Bali)** | 2007 | **Bali Action Plan**: Introduced Nationally Appropriate Mitigation Actions (NAMAs) for developing nations and REDD+ (Reducing Emissions from Deforestation and forest Degradation). |
| **COP15 (Copenhagen)**| 2009 | Non-binding Copenhagen Accord. First political pledge of **$100 billion per year by 2020** in climate finance for developing nations. |
| **COP16 (Cancún)** | 2010 | Formalized the establishment of the **Green Climate Fund (GCF)**. |
| **COP17 (Durban)** | 2011 | Decided to formulate a universal legal agreement by 2015 applicable to all countries (the Durban Platform). |
| **COP18 (Doha)** | 2012 | Doha Amendment establishing the 2nd commitment period of Kyoto Protocol (2013–2020). |
| **COP19 (Warsaw)** | 2013 | Established the **Warsaw International Mechanism for Loss and Damage**. |
| **COP21 (Paris)** | 2015 | **Paris Agreement**: Legally binding pact to keep temperature rise well below **2.0°C**, striving for **1.5°C**. Introduced **Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs)** submitted every 5 years. |
| **COP26 (Glasgow)**| 2021 | **Glasgow Climate Pact**: First explicit COP mention of phase-down of unabated coal power. Article 6 carbon markets rulebook finalized. India announced **Panchamrit** and Net-Zero by 2070. |
| **COP27 (Sharm el-Sheikh)** | 2022 | Landmark agreement to establish an operational **Loss and Damage Fund** for vulnerable nations. |
| **COP28 (Dubai)** | 2023 | Concluded the **First Global Stocktake (GST)**. UAE Consensus called for "transitioning away from fossil fuels in energy systems". Global Goal on Adaptation framework adopted. |

## India's Five Commitments (Panchamrit at COP26)
1. Reach **500 GW non-fossil energy capacity** by 2030.
2. Meet **50% of energy requirements from renewables** by 2030.
3. Reduce projected carbon emissions by **1 billion tonnes** between 2021 and 2030.
4. Reduce carbon intensity of GDP by **over 45%** by 2030 from 2005 levels.
5. Achieve the target of **Net-Zero emissions by 2070**.

## Core Principles to Remember in Answers
- **Common But Differentiated Responsibilities and Respective Capabilities (CBDR-RC)**: Core principle of climate justice reflecting historical responsibility of developed nations.
- **Climate Finance Credibility**: The unfulfilled promise of predictable, non-debt-creating climate financing remains the primary dispute between the Global North and Global South.
